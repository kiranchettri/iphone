FAQ

AI image detector bypass questions.

What does Forensic Cleaner do?

Forensic Cleaner reduces detector-facing signals while keeping the result close to the original image.

Results vary by image and detector. No method is guaranteed to bypass every detector.

Can I make an AI image undetectable?

No tool can honestly promise that an AI image is undetectable by every detector. What Forensic Cleaner does is reduce the detector-facing signals those tools score, so the cleaned image is much less likely to be flagged. Internal testing puts the clean rate at about 90% across three popular AI image detectors, and results vary by image and detector.

To give an image the best chance, start with Strong, turn on SynthID Removal for Gemini or Imagen files, and test the exported file rather than the original.

Which cleaning strength should I use?

Start with Strong for the best chance of passing on the first attempt.

If Strong works but changes the image more than you would like, try Quality. Quality mode uses a gentler process and makes the cleaning effect less visible.

Why is my cleaned image still being detected?

First, confirm that you are testing the cleaned output rather than the original image.

Detection rates are not 100%, and results vary widely between detectors. Some are simply unreliable: they routinely flag real photographs as AI, because their scores react to resolution and JPEG compression rather than to how the image was actually made.

For those detectors, try the 1024px downscale option. Downscaling can improve results and can also improve SynthID removal reliability.

When generating your images, prompt for "hyperrealistic amateur photos" for the best results.

What strength should I use for SynthID removal?

Medium is a good starting point.

If small or distant faces become distorted, use a lower strength. Higher settings are more aggressive but can have a larger effect on image quality.

Does Two Tensors remove hidden metadata from images?

Yes. The current workflow removes EXIF, IPTC, XMP, C2PA manifests, Content Credentials, and other AI provenance metadata from the exported image.

What is the maximum supported resolution?

The current maximum output size is approximately 2 megapixels. Higher resolutions are not presently available because of processing and GPU requirements.
